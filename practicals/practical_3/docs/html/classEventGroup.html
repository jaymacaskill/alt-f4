Detailed Description

A composite grouping of EventComponents (e.g. the main hall).

EventGroup is the Composite role in the Composite pattern and participates in Observer in two distinct roles: as an Observer, it receives notices from the Subject it is registered with (parent or ControlDesk), as a Subject, it rebroadcasts notices to its own children, producing the cascading notification required across the event tree
